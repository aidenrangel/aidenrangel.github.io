Chain of custody

Five steps, kept in order.

The sequence is the security. No drive skips a step; no step closes without a record.

FIRST

Intake & Audit

Serial numbers logged, assets tagged. Custody opens the moment your hardware reaches our hands.

SECOND

Secure Wipe

Multi-pass KillDisk sanitization across a 240-slot bench, monitored in real time.

THIRD

Verification

Full-surface read-back. Every sector returns zero, or the drive is sent to the shredder.

FOURTH

Certification

A signed certificate per drive — serial, method, result. Your audit trail, closed.

FIFTH

Recovery or Recycling

Sanitized drives re-enter the market; everything else is responsibly recycled.
