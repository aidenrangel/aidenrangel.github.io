Complimentary Program

Certified data destruction at no cost

Through our complimentary destruction program, Caliism performs professional, certified data sanitization using industry-leading KillDisk technology — at no charge — when sanitized drives are retained by Caliism after verification.

Your organization receives full compliance documentation and a closed audit trail. There is no invoice.

What's included

Multi-pass sanitization — professional KillDisk wiping to NIST 800-88 standards
Full verification — every sector confirmed; failed drives are physically destroyed
Per-drive certification — serial number, method, and result on every certificate
Zero cost — the service is free when Caliism retains the sanitized hardware
Our Process

A documented chain of custody, start to finish

Every drive follows the same five audited steps. No step is skipped, and no step closes without a record.

1

Intake & Audit

Serial numbers logged and assets tagged. Chain of custody opens on arrival.

2

Secure Wipe

Multi-pass KillDisk sanitization across 240 monitored wipe slots.

3

Verification

Full-surface read-back confirms complete sanitization. Failures are shredded.

4

Certification

A per-drive certificate documents serial, method, and verification result.

5

Recovery & Recycling

Sanitized drives are remarketed; all other material is responsibly recycled.
