Data Destruction

When data has to be gone, "deleted" isn't a standard — verified is. Caliism sanitizes storage media by software wipe or physical destruction, and documents both to the same audit-ready standard.

Two paths to provably gone

Software sanitization is our default for healthy drives. Using KillDisk Industrial across a 240-slot wipe bench, we run multi-pass overwrites to NIST 800-88 and DoD 5220.22-M standards, then perform a full-surface read-back to verify every sector returns zero. Wiped drives keep their resale value — which is what makes our free destruction program possible.

Physical destruction handles everything else: drives that fail verification, failed or degraded media that can't be reliably wiped, and any media your policy requires destroyed regardless of condition. Shredding and hydraulic crushing reduce drives to fragments that no laboratory recovers data from.

Either path ends the same way — with a per-drive certificate listing the serial number, method used, date, and verification result. Your auditors get a complete, defensible record; you get to stop thinking about those drives forever.

From live floor to empty room, on schedule

Caliism runs the project end to end. What leaves the building is documented. What had data on it is certified destroyed. What still has value comes back to you as recovered budget through our asset recovery program.

Asset Recovery

Retired hardware isn't waste — it's an asset class your books forgot about. We test, grade, and remarket end-of-life equipment so it comes back to you as budget instead of a disposal invoice.

Idle racks are stranded capital

Servers, switches, drives, and infrastructure that no longer serve your environment still hold real market value — but only if they're tested, graded, and sold through the right channels before that value decays. Most organizations either pay to dispose of it or let it depreciate in a storage cage.

Caliism handles the whole recovery cycle. Equipment is functionally tested and cosmetically graded; data-bearing devices are sanitized and certified through our destruction process first, so nothing leaves with your data on it. Then we remarket through our worldwide distribution footprint to maximize realized value.

The result: a documented return on hardware you'd already written off, with the same chain-of-custody rigor as everything else we do.

Relocation & Migration

Moving a data center is a thousand small risks riding in the same truck. Caliism plans, transports, and re-commissions your environment with serial-level tracking from the old floor to the new one.

The move is the easy part. The plan is everything.

A successful relocation is decided before anything is unplugged. We start with a full inventory and dependency map, sequence the shutdown to minimize downtime windows, and stage the destination site so equipment goes from truck to rack without sitting on a dock.

In transit, every asset is tracked by serial against the inventory. At the destination, our deployment crews re-rack, re-cable, and bring the environment back up to the documented plan — the same teams that handle our greenfield deployments.

If part of the environment isn't making the move, it flows into our decommissioning and asset recovery processes instead — one project plan covers the whole transition, with nothing falling between vendors.
